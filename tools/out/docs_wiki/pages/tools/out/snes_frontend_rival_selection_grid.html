SNES Front-End Rival Selection Grid

  • Claim: L00C20B owns a verified downstream 4-state 2x2 front-end surface on $1C70, with three OAM car cells plus a separate BG1 stopwatch/clock slot.
  • Scope: promote the structural read of the post-car, pre-track selector surface without over-promoting the unresolved fourth cell.

  • Hidden callback: 01:C1D2
  • Selection-box row: 1E:8000 row 0x1D
  • Selector field: $1C70
